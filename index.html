Why this works

Four things most video shops can’t offer.

01

It’s built in code, not on a timeline

Every frame is a program. Changing a colour, a number, a language or an aspect ratio is an edit and a re-render, not a rebuild — which is why revisions come back the same day and variants cost almost nothing.

02

Correct by default

The explainers are made by someone who reads the docs. Nothing gets hand-waved into a pretty animation that’s quietly wrong, which matters when your audience is engineers who will notice.

03

Nothing is a template

Every piece gets its own palette, layout, motion language and hero object, designed from your concept. No house style dropped over your logo, because recycled work is obvious to exactly the people you want to impress.

04

You see it before you commit

If the brief is specific, you get ten to fifteen seconds of your actual concept, built and sent for free. Judge the work, not the pitch.

Questions

The things people ask first.

DeliverablesWhat do I actually receive?

Finished video files — MP4, H.264, in whatever aspect ratios you need (9:16, 1:1, 16:9), plus an SRT subtitle file and a poster frame for thumbnails. Editable project files aren’t included: the animation is built in a custom code pipeline rather than After Effects, so there’s no timeline to hand over. If you need something changed later, send it back — that’s usually faster than editing it yourself would be.

TimelineHow long does it take?

A first short is about a week, because that’s where the visual identity gets built. After that, two to three days each. Long-form runs two to three weeks depending on length and how much research it needs.

ScriptingWho writes the script?

I do, unless you’d rather. Most clients send a rough brief or a doc and get a script back for approval before anything is animated. If you already have a script and voiceover, that works too and it’s faster.

RevisionsWhat if I don’t like it?

One revision round is included on every project, and script approval happens before any animation starts, so surprises are rare by design. For a first project the spec sample means you’ve already seen the treatment applied to your own concept before committing.

Brand fitCan you match our brand?

Yes — send your palette, type and any guidelines and the piece is designed inside them. What doesn’t get reused is the layout and motion language; those are built fresh for each concept.

PricingHow does pricing work?

Per project, quoted on scope. Length, research depth, number of variants and turnaround all move it, so a rate card would be misleading. Tell me what you need and you’ll get a number, not a package.
